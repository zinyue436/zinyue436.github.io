About Me

I am a master's student in Biomedical Data Science at Nanyang Technological University, Singapore. Previously, I received my BSc in Biological Sciences from Shandong University.

My research focuses on computational pathology, multimodal biomedical AI, and evidence-guided agents. I am interested in how AI systems find relevant visual evidence, reason across image scales, and connect their answers to inspectable findings.

My recent work includes EviPathBench, which evaluates evidence acquisition and reasoning in whole-slide pathology, and a multiscale agent for lung whole-slide image analysis. My background also spans spatial transcriptomics and microbial genomics.

Research Experience

Apr – Sep 2026

An Evidence-Guided Multiscale Agent for Lung Whole-Slide Image Analysis

Designed and implemented a pathology agent combining vision-language models, region retrieval, and dynamic tool calling for question-guided slide browsing and high-magnification inspection.

Linked answers to regional coordinates, histomorphological observations, supporting images, and uncertainty. Evaluated the agent on 1,208 questions across 799 TCGA and HISTAI slides.

Whole-slide navigationTool-using agentsVisual grounding

Feb – Aug 2026

EviPathBench: Evidence Acquisition and Reasoning

Conducted experiments with 19 vision-language models and one text-only reference model across matching, retrieval, localization, and multiscale reasoning tasks. Analyzed the gap between reasoning from supplied observations and locating visual evidence, including error propagation during multiscale search.

Model evaluationMultiscale reasoningError analysis

Spatial Transcriptomics of Tumor–Immune Organization

Processed multi-sample Visium data with Seurat, annotated dominant cell populations, and used nearest-neighbor analysis to study tumor–immune proximity across treatment groups, accounting for mixed-cell spots and incomplete response annotations.
